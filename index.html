使用说明

  • 打开后用前置摄像头当镜子，画面左右翻转，和照镜子一样。
  • 需要亮一点时，点底栏灯泡打开补光。屏幕四周会亮起来。长按灯泡可选暖光、日光、冷白、柔粉。
  • 双指缩放看细节。点画面可定格，再点继续。
  • 「抠图」打开后：灯关着会把房间糊掉，灯开着会换成灯色背景。
  • 想看更清晰的画面，或照到耳朵后面、后颈时，把另一台 iOS 设备的后置镜头无线连过来。画面只发到眼前这台，不会上传。
  • 含 720p。1080p 60 帧和 4K 30 帧可一次购买解锁，以拍摄设备的镜头能够达到的为准。
  • 不拍照、不录像、不进相册。关掉应用后画面就没有了。

隐私政策

当前版本（2.0）不收集、不上传个人数据。无账号、无广告、无第三方统计。

补光颜色、抠图和倍率等开关只存在这台设备上。卸载应用后一并删除。

一台设备时，相机只在屏幕上显示前置画面，当镜子用。连接另一台设备时，后置镜头的画面只通过本地网络发到眼前这台，不会发到服务器，也不会保存。没扫过码的设备解不开画面。

购买由 Apple 处理。本应用不接收银行卡信息。

如需撤回相机或本地网络权限，请到系统设置 › 光言灯镜。

Guangyan Mirror

Front-camera makeup mirror. You can also connect another device's rear camera. For iPhone and iPad.

Developer: Yu Guan　Support: findkevin@163.com

Support

For questions, App Review notes, or feature requests, email the address above.

How to use

  • The front camera is a live mirror. The picture is flipped left-to-right, like a real mirror.
  • Tap the bulb to turn on fill light around the frame. Hold the bulb to pick warm, daylight, cool, or soft pink.
  • Pinch to zoom. Tap the picture to freeze it, tap again to continue.
  • Cutout: with the light off, the room blurs; with the light on, the background becomes the lamp color.
  • For a sharper picture, or to see behind an ear or the back of the neck, wirelessly connect another iOS device's rear camera. The picture goes only to the device in front of you and is not uploaded.
  • 720p is included. Unlock 1080p at 60 fps and 4K at 30 fps once. Up to what the camera on the shooting device can do.
  • Nothing is photographed, recorded, or saved to Photos. Closing the app clears the picture.

Privacy policy

Version 2.0 does not collect or upload personal data. No accounts, ads, or analytics.

Fill-light color, cutout, and zoom stay on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

On one device, the camera is only a live mirror of the front camera. When another device is connected, that rear-camera picture is sent only over the local network to the device in front of you. It is not sent to a server and is not saved. A device that did not scan the code cannot open the picture.

Purchases are handled by Apple. This app does not receive card details.

To revoke camera or local network access, go to Settings › Guangyan Mirror.
